## Node.js 24

`@dudousxd/nestjs-filter` 2.x (with `-client`, `-mikro-orm` and `-typeorm`) and the 0.x adapters released alongside it
(`-clickhouse`, `-codegen`, `-drizzle`, `-memory`, `-react`) require **Node.js 24 or newer**.

Upgrade the Node.js version your app runs on (and builds with), then upgrade the packages. The API did not change.
Under `engine-strict`, installing on Node 20 or 22 now fails.
